What to ask each company

  1. Is the price you quote the installed price, including plumbing, electrical and tax?
  2. Which products and brands, and who services them in Canada?
  3. What is the warranty term, in writing, for labour and for materials?
  4. How many days will the bathroom be out of use?
  5. Do you remove and dispose of the old fixtures, and is that in the quote?
  6. Can I speak to two customers from the last year?
