What published prices leave out - ask each company

  1. Is the permit included, and who pulls it?
  2. Does the price cover moving plumbing or drains?
  3. Which tile or wall system is in the base price, and what is the upgrade cost?
  4. Is disposal of the old tub and debris included?
  5. Is HST included in the figure you quoted?
