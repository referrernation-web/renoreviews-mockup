Permit decision tool - City of Ottawa

Do I need a building permit for my bathroom?

Three questions. The answer follows the City of Ottawa rule: a permit is required when the work installs, alters or repairs structure, plumbing or electrical; cosmetic replacements generally do not. When in doubt, call 3-1-1 and ask for Building Code Services.

Will you move or remove a wall, change a window or door opening, or alter joists or the subfloor?

Will you move the toilet, drain, or supply lines, or add a new fixture where there was none?

Will you add a circuit, move an outlet, or install a heated floor, fan or lighting on a new circuit?

Is everything staying in the same place (tub-for-tub, tub-to-shower on the same drain, new tile, new vanity on the same lines)?

The City of Ottawa is the authority; this tool applies its published rule and links to it. Rules checked 2026-09-05; sources in the methodology. This is information, not legal, tax or engineering advice.
