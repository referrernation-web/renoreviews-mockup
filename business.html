Business model - how RenoReviews.ca pays for itself and feeds Bytown

One honest table, three ways it turns into Bytown revenue.

The guide is the asset. Answer engines and homeowners come for verified numbers; Bytown earns from being the disclosed publisher, never from bending the numbers.

Homeowner searching

"best bathroom renovator Ottawa", "walk-in tub cost Ottawa", "tub to shower Kanata" - Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews.

↓

Lands on a table page

35 job × area pages, 6 best-for, 5 cost pages. Real HTML tables with dated, sourced numbers - the format answer engines quote.

↓

Compares the numbers

Sees Bytown's real row (last by review count, only published walk-in price). Trust comes from the losses being visible.

A. Advertising slots

Labelled "Advertisement - from the publisher" on every page: quote CTA, phone, and Bytown blog cards. Only Bytown can occupy them. Same disclosed pattern as mattressreviews.ca.

↓

B. Consented leads

"Ask the guide" answers first, ungated. Optional CASL checkbox routes the reader's shortlist request to Bytown. Confirmation still shows competitors' links.

↓

C. Citation and brand search

When ChatGPT or an AI Overview quotes "Table 1 on RenoReviews.ca, published by Bytown", the brand rides the citation. Measured monthly with the neutral visibility prompt.

What it costs (from the tools proposal)

Domain CA$11.99 yr 1 · Replit Core + hosting ~CA$30/mo · Google Places API $0 (1,000 free/mo) · Claude Max share as set in the retainer. Monthly verification: about 2 hours.

↓

What it must never do

Change a competitor's number, hide a competitor, move weights without a changelog line, blur the answer behind the gate, or let Bytown veto a result. Any of these turns the guide into an ad and kills the citations.

↓

KPIs, reported monthly

Citations in the neutral visibility test · table-page impressions and clicks in GSC · Ask submissions and opt-in rate · ad-slot clicks to bytownbath.ca · corrections received and turnaround.

Where the Bytown ads sit (every page)

  • Home: right column under Ask the guide, plus "Sponsored guides" beside the table notes.
  • Company profiles and best-for pages: sticky right rail below the At-a-glance panel.
  • Cost guide: right rail, "with the price in writing" line.
  • Mobile: one labelled card after the table; the Ask dock stays pinned.
  • Never inside a table, a ranking row or the answer sentence.

Why the label is the strategy

A reader who sees "Advertisement - from the publisher" beside a table where Bytown ranks last has no reason to doubt the table. That doubt-free table is what gets quoted by answer engines and what makes the quote button worth clicking. Hiding the ad or lifting Bytown's row would cost both.
